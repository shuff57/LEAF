Plant identification, on your terms

Point a photo at a plant. Start with a better lead.

L.E.A.F. is a small, self-hosted plant identification service. Choose a photo and compare the plants a model thinks it sees, either through your own server or directly in a browser.

Run it where the photo makes sense.

One interface, two useful paths.

Use a self-hosted model when you want a service you control. When browser-ready models are present, L.E.A.F. can run them on the device that took the photo, keeping that image in the browser.

  • Self-hostedFastAPI serves the page and swappable plant-identification models from the same container.
  • On-deviceBrowser models use WebGPU when available, with a WebAssembly fallback and local browser caching.
  • CompareWhen several ready models are available, run them side by side and see where they differ.
