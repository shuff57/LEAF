The workbench

Lightweight Engine for Assessing Flora. Add a plant photo, choose a model, and inspect its ranked matches.

ModelDownloadMemory

Drop a photo here, paste one, or choose a file.

Add a photo, then choose Identify. The closest matches appear here.

Scores show how a model splits its confidence among the plants it knows. They are not the chance that the answer is right.

A photo match is a lead, not a verdict. Confirm with a field guide or a local expert, and never eat or handle a plant based on this result alone.

On-device models: BioCLIP 2 and BioCLIP 2.5 by Imageomics, and BioCLIP 2.5 Mobile by Nate Hamilton, all MIT licensed. Common names from iNaturalist.
